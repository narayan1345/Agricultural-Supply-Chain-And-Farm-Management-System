# AgriTrack: Agricultural Supply Chain & Farm Management System

## Introduction
Agriculture involves farmers, suppliers, warehouses, distributors and retailers. Fragmented records can lead to inefficient inventory management, poor coordination, distribution delays and limited visibility. AgriTrack is a centralized, database-driven platform that integrates farm management with supply chain operations.

## Problem Statement
Traditional agricultural supply chains often rely on disconnected record-keeping methods, making it difficult to track crop production, manage resources, monitor inventory and coordinate distribution. Farmers may struggle to connect with buyers, while distributors and retailers lack reliable stock visibility. An integrated system is needed to maintain accurate records, streamline transactions and improve transparency among stakeholders.

## Proposed Solution
AgriTrack connects farmers, suppliers, warehouses and distributors through a unified platform. It supports farm registration, crop tracking, harvest recording, inventory monitoring, order processing and delivery management. Products can be tracked from cultivation and harvest through storage and distribution.

## Key Modules
Farmer and farm management; crop production; supplier management; harvest recording; warehouse and inventory management; procurement and orders; delivery tracking; and activity logs.

## DBMS Concepts
The project uses MySQL for relational data and MongoDB for flexible farm activity logs. It demonstrates ER modelling, normalization, keys and constraints, DDL/DML, joins, nested and correlated queries, recursive CTEs, views, stored procedures, functions, triggers, transactions and MongoDB Create/Read/Update/Delete operations.

## Expected Outcome
The system provides centralized agricultural records, improves inventory visibility, simplifies procurement and order management, and demonstrates the practical use of relational and NoSQL databases in an agricultural supply chain.
